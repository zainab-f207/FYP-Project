# 📸 How to Get Better OCR Results

## 🎯 Quick Wins for Your Document

### Step 1: Crop the Image

Your document has QR codes and watermarks that confuse the OCR. Here's how to improve:

**Remove:**
- QR codes (top-left and top-right corners)
- "Scanned with CamScanner" watermark (bottom)
- Extra white space around edges

**Keep:**
- Main text content
- Table data

### Step 2: Process Sections Separately

Instead of uploading the whole page, try uploading:

1. **Header section** (top table with dates and numbers)
2. **Main table** (middle section with Urdu text)
3. **Body paragraph** (bottom Urdu text)

This gives MUCH better results!

### Step 3: Enhance the Image

Before uploading:
1. Increase **contrast** (make text darker, background whiter)
2. Increase **brightness** if needed
3. Save as **high-quality PNG** (not compressed JPEG)

## 🖼️ Using Built-in Windows Tools

### Method 1: Windows Photos App

1. Open image in **Photos** app
2. Click **Edit & Create** → **Edit**
3. Adjust **Light** slider to increase contrast
4. Click **Crop** to remove QR codes
5. Save as new file

### Method 2: Paint

1. Open image in **Paint**
2. Use **Select** tool to crop out QR codes
3. Save as PNG

### Method 3: Online Tools

- **remove.bg** - Remove watermarks
- **photopea.com** - Free Photoshop alternative
- **iloveimg.com** - Crop and enhance

## 📊 Expected Results

**Before (full page with QR codes):**
- Confidence: 30-50%
- Text: Mostly gibberish

**After (cropped main text only):**
- Confidence: 60-85%
- Text: Much more readable

## 🎯 Best Practice Workflow

1. **Crop** the image to remove QR codes and watermarks
2. **Enhance** contrast and brightness
3. **Upload** to OCR system
4. **Extract** text
5. **Manually correct** any errors

## 💡 Pro Tip

For tables, try uploading each **row** separately. This often gives perfect results because the OCR can focus on one line at a time.
