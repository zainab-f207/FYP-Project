## 🎯 Why EasyOCR?
- **Free & Open Source**: No subscription, no limits.
- **Deep Learning**: Uses advanced AI models to recognize text.
- **Better Urdu Support**: Specifically trained on complex languages.
- **No API Keys**: Runs entirely on your local machine.

## 🔄 What Happens Next?
1. Once the installation finishes, I will **restart the backend server**.
